# MSc Research – Geographical Bias in Pedestrian Detection

## Project Identity

- **Researcher:** Tonu James
- **Official research title:** Investigating Regional Bias in CNN-based Pedestrian Detection: Europe vs India
- **Academic context:** MSc in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence, TU Dublin, completed February 2025
- **Research areas:** Computer Vision, Pedestrian Detection, AI Fairness, Geographical Bias
- **Project type:** MSc research thesis

## Research Question

Does a CNN-based pedestrian detection system show different detection performance when evaluated on pedestrian scenes from India and Europe?

## Motivation

Computer-vision models can face differences in camera viewpoints, scene density, road layout, lighting, pedestrian appearance, and other environmental conditions.

Evaluating model behaviour across different geographical settings can help identify limitations in generalization and fairness.

## Methodological Overview

Tonu investigated the performance of pedestrian detection approaches across Indian and European conditions, comparing detection outcomes to understand geographical disparities.

The thesis also connected model performance evaluation with fairness and responsible AI considerations.

## Research Finding

The research identified a meaningful geographical performance disparity between Indian and European pedestrian-detection conditions, emphasizing the importance of evaluating visual AI on representative environments rather than assuming equivalent behaviour across regions.

## Relevance

The project demonstrates Tonu's academic experience in computer vision, comparing model performance, measuring detection outcomes, and assessing fairness across different conditions.
