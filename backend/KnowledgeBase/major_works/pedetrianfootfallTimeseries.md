# MSc Academic Project – Pedestrian Footfall Time-Series Forecasting

## Project Identity

- **Researcher:** Tonu James
- **Project title:** A Comparative Study of Pedestrian Footfall Trends Using Time Series Forecasting
- **Academic context:** MSc in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence, TU Dublin
- **Research areas:** Time Series, Forecasting, Statistical Modelling, Comparative Evaluation
- **Project type:** Academic research

## Problem and Objective

The project investigated approaches for forecasting trends in pedestrian footfall, with an emphasis on comparing different time-series forecasting methods.

## Methods Considered

- **Holt-Winters:** Forecasting approach that can model level, trend, and seasonality.
- **SARIMA:** Seasonal autoregressive integrated moving-average modelling.
- **Prophet:** A forecasting framework designed for trend and seasonality modelling.

## Contribution

Tonu worked on this academic comparison as part of his MSc studies.

## Research Scope

The academic work compared Holt-Winters, SARIMA, and Prophet approaches for forecasting pedestrian footfall trends.
