# About Tonu James — Professional Profile

## Who is Tonu James?
Tonu James is a software engineering professional transitioning into applied AI, data science and AI engineering. He completed a **Master of Science in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence at Technological University Dublin (TU Dublin)** in 2025, with **Second Class Honours**.

His previous career involved enterprise software development at **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)** and **SAP Labs India**, totalling close to nine years of industry software engineering experience. This is primarily professional **software engineering**, not a claim of nine years of commercial AI engineering.

## Enterprise software background
At SAP Labs India, Tonu developed, integrated and supported enterprise applications in banking, financial services and product-lifecycle management. He served as the technical lead of a **four-member team** supporting the SAP Multi-Bank Connectivity Connector for **more than 100 customer organisations**. His earlier TCS role involved Java and financial risk-reporting-related functionality.

## Education and research interests
Tonu holds a **Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering**, a **Master of Technology in Computer Science with Image Processing** and a **Master of Science in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence**.

His research includes pedestrian detection fairness using YOLOX, Dublin pedestrian footfall forecasting with SARIMA/Holt-Winters/Prophet, face similarity with Siamese networks and KNN (associated with a 2024 ACM conference poster), and earlier video steganography work.
