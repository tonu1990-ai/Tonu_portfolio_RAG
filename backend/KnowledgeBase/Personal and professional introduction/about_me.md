# About Tonu James

## Professional Profile

Tonu James is a software engineering professional transitioning into AI engineering.

Based in Ireland and originally from Kerala, India, he has close to nine years of enterprise software development experience, primarily at Tata Consultancy Services and SAP Labs India.

His professional background includes SAP enterprise application development, customer-specific customization, integration, technical support, and team leadership.

At SAP Labs, he led a four-member team supporting the SAP Multi-Bank Connectivity Connector for more than 100 customer organizations.

## Education and AI Focus

Tonu holds a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, an M.Tech in Computer Science with Image Processing, and an MSc in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence from Technological University Dublin.

His academic and applied interests include computer vision, deep learning, AI fairness, Edge AI, generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and agentic AI.

## Current Projects and Professional Direction

Tonu has a fully deployed and operational personal portfolio with a RAG-powered chatbot that answers questions using information from his professional knowledge base.

He has also developed a functioning Raspberry Pi Edge AI application with camera preview, YOLO inference, object detection display, and Docker deployment.

He continues strengthening his skills through hands-on projects and learning and is equally interested in Generative AI/LLM Engineering, Computer Vision/Edge AI, and Enterprise AI/SAP Generative AI roles.

He aims to build reliable, useful, and responsible AI applications grounded in sound software engineering practices.

## Personal Note

Tonu is married and is a father of three children.
