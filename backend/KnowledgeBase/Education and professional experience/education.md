### M.Tech Research – Video Steganography

- **Research title:** Hiding Images and Video inside Video using Message Dependent Key Based Encryption and Entropy Based Hiding
- **Research areas:** Image Processing, Encryption, Video Steganography, Information Security
- **Publication:** IIJC, Volume 4, Issue 3, July 2014

The research explored encrypting secret image or video content using a message-dependent key and concealing it within a cover video using local entropy-based hiding.

The goal was to investigate secure information hiding and efficient use of cover-video capacity.

Further technical details belong in `major_works/ImageSteganography.md`.
