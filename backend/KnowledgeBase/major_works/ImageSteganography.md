# M.Tech Research – Video Steganography and Encryption

## Project Identity

- **Researcher:** Tonu James
- **Project title:** Hiding Images and Video inside Video using Message Dependent Key Based Encryption and Entropy Based Hiding
- **Academic context:** M.Tech in Computer Science with Image Processing, CUSAT, 2012–2014
- **Research areas:** Image Processing, Encryption, Video Steganography, Information Hiding
- **Publication:** IIJC, Volume 4, Issue 3, July 2014
- **Project type:** Academic research

## Problem and Objective

The research addressed concealing secret image or video content within a cover video while considering information security and the use of available video capacity.

## Approach

The proposed method combined:

- A message-dependent key-based encryption stage for secret image or video data.
- An entropy-based embedding approach to conceal encrypted information within a high-resolution cover video.
