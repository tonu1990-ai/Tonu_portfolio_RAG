### MSc research thesis
- Title on submitted thesis cover: **Fairness in Pedestrian Detection: A Comparative Study of Regional and Demographic Bias Using YOLOX**.
- Focus: Evaluation of regional and demographic differences in YOLOX pedestrian-detection miss rates between selected European and Indian-condition street-scene datasets.
- Original data preparation: Created/curated a final Indian-condition research dataset comprising **2,173 images** and **5,416 evaluated pedestrian bounding boxes**.
- Main outcome: YOLOX miss rates of **28.27%** for the Indian-condition dataset and **6.56%** for the European comparison dataset, within the reported research setting.
- Full project: `major_works/pedestrianDetectionBias.md`.

### Other MSc projects
- **Dublin footfall forecasting:** Analysed Dublin City Council pedestrian sensor records and compared SARIMA, Prophet and Holt-Winters; SARIMA reported the lowest RMSE (**24,163**) among tested configurations. Details: `major_works/pedetrianfootfallTimeseries.md`.
- **Face similarity and explainability:** Compared custom and VGG19-based Siamese models with KNN; associated ACM conference poster published in 2024. Details: `major_works/SiameseKNNFaceVerification.md`.
