# M.Tech Research: Video Steganography and Encryption

## Project identity
- Researcher: Tonu James.
- Research title: Hiding Images and Video inside Video using Message Dependent Key Based Encryption and Entropy Based Hiding.
- Programme: Master of Technology in Computer Science with Image Processing, Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), India, 2012–2014.
- Work type: M.Tech academic research in image processing, cryptography-related methods, information hiding and video steganography.
- Reported publication: IIJC, Volume 4, Issue 3, July 2014.

## Research question and objective
The project investigated how to conceal secret image or video information inside another video (the cover video). The aim was to combine confidentiality of the secret content with a method for selecting suitable areas of the cover video for information hiding.

## Method and key concepts
- **Message-dependent key-based encryption:** The secret image or video information was encrypted before concealment using a key derived in relation to the message.
- **Entropy-based hiding:** Local entropy characteristics of a high-resolution cover video were considered when selecting where to embed encrypted information.
- **Cover video:** The video that carries the concealed content.
- **Secret payload:** The image or video content being encrypted and hidden.
- **Video steganography:** Concealing the existence of the payload within visual media, whereas encryption protects its readable content.

## Tonu's contribution and outcome
Tonu undertook this research during his M.Tech studies. The research proposed combining message-dependent encryption with entropy-guided embedding of secret image/video content into cover video. An associated paper was reported as published in IIJC in July 2014.
